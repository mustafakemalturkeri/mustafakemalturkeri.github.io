# Mustafa Kemal Türkeri

Water resources engineer based in Ankara, Türkiye. I work on river basin planning, hydrological modelling, drought and climate change assessment, and I build the software and data tools behind that work.

- Kızılırmak Basin Lead in the EU-funded *Technical Assistance on Preparation of River Basin Management Plans for Six Basins* (6RBMP), prepared for the Directorate General for Water Management under the EU Water Framework Directive.
- More than 12 years of basin, drought and climate projects for DSİ, İSKİ, KASKİ, the World Bank and the Asian Development Bank.
- Co-founder of [Mecra Water and Environmental Technologies](https://mecra.tech).
- PhD student in Agricultural Structures and Irrigation at Ankara University; MSc and BSc in Civil Engineering from METU.
